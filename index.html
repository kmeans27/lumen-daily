How to play

  • A beam of light enters the grid from one edge.
  • Tap an empty cell to place a mirror; tap again to rotate it.
  • You get exactly the mirrors you need — no spares. Fewer moves = a cleaner solve.
  • Light up every target. A colored target must be hit by a beam of that color.
  • Prisms split the beam; filters recolor it. They’re fixed fixtures — don’t place them.

The light is the feedback: every tap re-runs the beam and shows you how close you are.
